02 / The person behind the work

A teacher's curiosity.
An analyst's perspective.

I’m Muhammad Fadli Azis, a lecturer and researcher in Electrical Engineering at Politeknik ATI Makassar.

I specialize in Power System Operations and Optimization. My research applies artificial intelligence, mathematical optimization, and uncertainty modeling to emerging and underexplored challenges in power system operations, renewable energy integration, and energy systems.

My interests span energy analytics and forecasting, including isolated microgrids. I have taught Power System Engineering in Machinery Systems Automation since January 2019.
